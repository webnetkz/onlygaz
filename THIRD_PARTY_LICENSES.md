# Third-party assets

## W16 Hypercar SFX Pack

All active game sound effects are from the user-supplied, procedurally
synthesised W16 Hypercar SFX Pack. Its audio may be used, modified and
redistributed in commercial and non-commercial projects without royalties or
attribution. The supplied Bevy integration code is MIT OR Apache-2.0.
The original terms are preserved at `vendor/w16_audio/LICENSE-SOURCE.txt`.
The existing radio tracks and menu music are separate from this pack.

## Vehicle brand logos

The vehicle-selection screen uses SVG versions of the manufacturers' official
brand marks for identification:

- Bugatti Macaron: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bugatti_logo.svg>
- Mercedes-Benz star: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mercedes-Benz_free_logo.svg>
- Pagani wordmark: <https://logo-teka.com/en/pagani/>, matched against the
  official Pagani site header at <https://www.pagani.com/>
- Lamborghini shield: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lamborghini_Logo.svg>
- Koenigsegg shield: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Koenigsegg_logo.svg>
- Ferrari shield: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ferrari-Logo.svg>
- Ford oval: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ford_Motor_Company_Logo.svg>

The Bugatti and Mercedes-Benz source pages identify these simple logo designs
as public-domain geometry/text. All three marks remain trademarks of their
respective owners; their inclusion identifies the simulated vehicles and does
not imply endorsement. The original vector paths are preserved under
`assets/ui/vehicle_logos/`.
