Conversion: tools/build_skybox.py. Six 2048x2048 faces in WebGPU order:
+X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z, stacked vertically. Pixel-centred bilinear projection,
wrapped longitude, identical exposure on every face, lossless RGB PNG.
All 12 edge pairs are checked against neighbouring interior pixel gradients.
The renderer explicitly uses linear filtering and clamp-to-edge cube sampling.
Downloaded source panoramas and edge-check reports: .asset_sources/sky/.
